# 访谈式创作｜访谈原则

Use this reference as an internal interview map. Do not paste it to the user as a full checklist during normal use. Pick the single most useful missing dimension and ask one question.

## Universal Opening

The first question is always:

> 你为什么想写这个选题？

Listen for:

- The emotional trigger: anger, regret, excitement, confusion, relief, ambition, shame, discovery.
- The strategic trigger: product, persona, public narrative, service sales, audience education, Build in Public, case study.
- The audience promise: who should care, and what they will understand after reading.
- The hidden stake: what happens if the user does not write this.

## Story Mode

Use when the user mentions an experience, a person, an event, a memory, a turning point, a failure, a conflict, or a lesson.

Check whether the material includes:

- Core conflict: What was the tension, contradiction, obstacle, or decision?
- Background: Who was involved, where did it happen, what was the situation?
- Details: What did the user see, say, do, hear, decide, misunderstand, or notice?
- Turns: What changed? Where was the high point? Where was the low point?
- Specificity: Are there numbers, examples, dates, scenes, stakes, comparisons?
- Meaning: What life insight, work principle, or long-term judgment emerged?
- Universality: Can a broad enough audience see themselves in the story?
- Oral clarity: If this came from speech, did the user speak too fast, skip words, or compress key moments?

Useful questions:

- "这件事里最冲突的地方是什么？"
- "故事开始前，你处在什么背景里？"
- "能不能讲一个当时的具体画面？"
- "中间有没有一个高光时刻，或者一个很糟糕的低谷？"
- "有没有数字、时间、金额、人数或对比，能让这个故事更具体？"
- "这件事最后让你对人生、工作或关系有什么新判断？"
- "你觉得哪类人会在这个故事里看到自己？"

## Opinion Mode

Use when the user wants to express a view, judgment, hot take, observation, complaint, critique, or belief.

Check whether the material includes:

- A claim sharp enough to create curiosity or resonance.
- A phrase or keyword that grabs attention without becoming clickbait.
- A structure that is either clear total-part-total, or starts by breaking common sense.
- Examples that prove the point and progress layer by layer.
- Natural connectors: "所以呢", "然后", "而且", "但是", "其实" can preserve oral flow when used naturally.
- An ending that returns to the claim and moves emotion forward.
- Avoidance of correct nonsense: no empty truth, no fake depth, no abstract language that people would not say.
- Unique examples or judgments that are not just copied from the internet.

Useful questions:

- "这个观点最反常识、最容易让人停下来看的一句话是什么？"
- "别人通常会怎么说这件事？你不同意哪里？"
- "你能不能给一个别人很难在网上抄到的例子？"
- "这个观点要层层推进的话，第一层、第二层、第三层分别是什么？"
- "结尾你想把读者的情绪推到哪里？更愤怒、更清醒、更有行动感，还是更释然？"
- "这里有没有一句听起来正确但其实没信息量的话？"

## Practical Insight Mode

Use when the user wants to teach a method, summarize a lesson, explain a framework, share a playbook, or provide a useful takeaway.

Check whether the material includes:

- One or two sentences that can express the core useful point.
- A real audience pain point.
- A method that the audience can understand and try.
- Low preaching: the tone should not sound like lecturing from above.
- A structure that explains what it is, why it matters, and how to do it.
- A complexity-to-simplicity path: first show why the issue is messy, then give the usable handle.
- A misconception-to-correction path: the logic may contrast wrong and right, but avoid the explicit overused phrase "不是……而是……" in final writing.
- A business, product, service, or conversion hook if natural.
- Concrete examples, metaphors, or scenes.

Useful questions:

- "如果这个干货只能压成一句话，你会怎么说？"
- "它直击的是哪类人的什么痛点？"
- "一个普通读者看完后，第一步能做什么？"
- "这里有没有说教感？你平时会怎么跟朋友讲？"
- "这个方法按‘是什么、为什么、怎么做’来拆，分别是什么？"
- "有没有一个很形象的例子，能让人一下子懂？"
- "这篇内容有没有自然提到你的业务或服务的地方？"

## Overall Expression

Across all modes, check:

- Audience: Is there a clear person being spoken to? Is the group large enough?
- Emotion: Is the piece emotionally flat, or does it have tension and movement?
- Naturalness: Does the user sound like a person, not a lesson plan or report?
- Pace: If sourced from speech, did they swallow words or speak too quickly?
- Product: Is there a place to introduce a product or service without damaging trust?
- Persona: Is there a place to strengthen the user's public character?
- Traffic opening: Is the topic framed broadly enough for more people to enter?

Useful questions:

- "你这篇最想讲给谁听？这个人现在卡在哪里？"
- "这件事里你的情绪是什么？平静、愤怒、兴奋、委屈，还是后怕？"
- "有没有哪句话太像正确废话，删掉也不影响意思？"
- "这篇文章能不能自然带出你的产品、服务或人设？"
- "如果想让更多人点进来，选题入口应该放大到哪个公共问题？"

## When to Stop Interviewing

Stop when:

- The user explicitly says writing can begin.
- The topic has a clear why, audience, tension, main claim, evidence, and ending direction.
- Further questioning would create friction rather than depth.

If the user asks to start before enough material exists, give a brief warning and offer a thin-but-usable structure:

> 现在可以写，但材料会偏薄。缺的是 [one missing thing]。我先给你一版故事线和逻辑线，你确认后我再写。
